CLOSEDLOOPBENCH 1.1 — BENCHMARK CARD
====================================
Temporal causal discovery on real clinical monitoring data with a clinician in the loop.
Version 1.1 = CLBench closed-loop benchmark v4 (runs/iclr/closed_loop_v4/): the 1.0 methods and admission rule
on a 2 s native grid for every VitalDB case, with 11 physics / physiology / policy relations added (29 in total).
Version 1.0 (runs/iclr/closed_loop_v3/) stays reproducible: ``use_table("v3")`` is the default and the v3 runner
path is unchanged. Code: src/clbench/bench/ (native_grid, loader, ground_truth, nulls, scoring, sim);
reference methods src/clbench/eval/closed_loop_bench.py; runner scripts/44_closed_loop_bench.py
(--build-grid, --bench-version v4); tests tests/unit/test_closed_loop_bench.py. This card is generated by
make_card.py from the run outputs.
Pre-registered protocol: protocol.md, sha256 418281f18a34c6c9c66111250b0d17d8f9296e8718c469a08515ba386793d10e (= registered hash)

1. WHAT 1.1 ADDS
----------------
An interpretive axis: relations whose sign is fixed by PHYSICS (ventilation -> EtCO2; inspired O2 ->
SpO2) against DRUG responses whose sign is fixed by pharmacology but which the clinician titrates in
response to the same vital signs (the drug titration paradox). The benchmark asks whether calibrated
methods recover the first and how they fare on the second; the answer is in section 5 (per-relation
method counts) and in the pre-specified predictions Q3-Q6.

2. DATA
-------
VitalDB (Lee HC et al., Sci Data 2022; vitaldb.net terms; nothing redistributed). 2 s grid on the
casestart clock built from the native 1 s numerics for all 3442 cases (clbench.bench.native_grid): MAP
(arterial line), HR, EtCO2, SpO2, BIS (SQI >= 50). Actions: propofol / remifentanil TCI targets,
ventilator set rate, phenylephrine / norepinephrine / nitroglycerin rates (v1-v3 events file); set tidal
volume, set PEEP, set FiO2 (native Primus set-point records); MAC steps DERIVED from the measured MAC
(the vaporiser dial is not recorded). Median coverage of 2 s bins: MAP 0.89, HR 0.94, EtCO2 0.91, SpO2
0.95, BIS 0.89.
Clock check: the events-file ventilator steps coincide with the native SET_RR changes without a shift
(3388 cases; median of case medians 0.50 s, the native 1 s rounding) and are off by the documented
loader offset with it (median 1.10 s), so no shift is applied.

4. NULLS AND METRICS
--------------------
As 1.0 (see runs/iclr/closed_loop_v3/BENCHMARK_CARD.txt, sections 4-5): nc_random plus K = 8 cross-
patient derangements; the calibrated admission rule (real minus mean of the 8 cross draws, SE with
between-draw variance, BY over the method's 29 relations, 75% draw stability, >= 5 cases); score =
correct - wrong admissions (range -29..29); cross leave-one-out and random-time false-admission rates.
New: a per-domain summary of the calibrated calls.

5b. READING THE RESULTS (post hoc notes, not pre-registered)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Ventilation -> EtCO2 (R01, R02, R13, R14) is admitted with the physical sign by every event design
(naive, matched, kink, seqtrial; naive_lvl 3 of 4) and by VAR-LiNGAM; VARX gets R13 wrong (4.2 [1.4,
7.1] per L). The level-based lag methods (cross-correlation, Granger) admit the set-rate relations
R01/R02 with the POLICY sign after calibration (xcorr 0.053 [0.044, 0.063], Granger 0.0019 [0.0013,
0.0025]): the clinician raises the rate when EtCO2 is high, and a lagged level association cannot
separate that from the response.
- FiO2 -> SpO2 below the ceiling (R15) has the physical sign in every event design but none admits it:
naive_lvl 1.04 [0.51, 1.56] SpO2 %, BY p 0.0007, stable in 0.62 of the draws (rule: 0.75); 510 cases
have an eligible event.
- Drug responses: at 2 s on all cases the hypnotic responses are admitted with the textbook sign by most
methods (propofol -> MAP R03, propofol -> BIS R19; derived MAC steps R17, R18), and the methods with
zero random-time false admissions (matched, kink, pcmciplus) admit no response relation with the wrong
sign. The titration paradox remains for remifentanil DECREASES: R07 (remi down -> HR up) is admitted
wrong-signed by naive, naive_lvl and VARX (0.22 [0.12, 0.32]); only Granger and VAR-LiNGAM (level-based,
see the random-time note) admit it with the textbook sign, no event design does; R08 is wrong for naive
/ naive_lvl and right for matched / kink. Vasoactive relations (R10-R12; 30-103 cases) are not admitted.
- The random-time null remains a different null: with nc_random as input the rule admits 16 (Granger),
12 (VAR-LiNGAM), 10-13 (cross-correlation) and 7 (naive) of 29 relations, and 0 for matched, kink and
PCMCI+, so the correct-sign admissions of the schedule-sensitive methods are weaker evidence.
- P10 (BIS rising -> MAC up) comes out wrong-signed for 8 methods. MAC steps are detected after the dial
change, so BIS is likely already falling before the detected step; we read this as a construct failure
of derived MAC events for policy relations (the lag was flagged in advance), not as evidence about
practice. P08 (EtCO2 rising -> set TV up) is wrong-signed for 6 methods: in these data set-TV increases
are preceded by falling EtCO2, so the assumed practice sign does not describe how set TV is changed here
(reason not examined). R16 (PEEP -> MAP) is not admitted by any method (sign right in 10 of 12, no
power).

6. INTENDED USE
---------------
As 1.0: evaluating temporal causal-discovery and knowledge-graph edge learners on closed-loop clinical
data. Not for clinical decision-making, dosing, effect sizes, or judging patients or clinicians.

7. LIMITATIONS (in addition to 1.0's)
-------------------------------------
- MAC steps are derived from a measured end-tidal concentration, which lags the dial and also moves with
fresh-gas flow and ventilation; MAC relations are exposure relations, not recorded actions.
- FiO2 steps cluster at induction and emergence; the event designs use only events with pre-level SpO2 <
97% (oximeter ceiling), the regression methods are unrestricted.
- PEEP steps are rare. Vasopressor relations have few cases, as in 1.0.
- PCMCI+ runs on a seeded subsample of 60 cases (30 s blocks, lags to 240 s), VAR-LiNGAM on at most
20,000 pooled residual rows, seqtrial with at most 10 control anchors per case and action (run time on a
shared machine; protocol §4).
- Sensitivity variants and the patient-specificity test were not recomputed (1.0 results stand).
- Physics signs are unambiguous, but the magnitude at a fixed horizon depends on CO2 / O2 stores; only
signs are scored.
